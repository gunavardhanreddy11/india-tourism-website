Trip finder

Find your kind of India

Answer three questions. Results update as you go, and nothing leaves your browser.

1 What are you in the mood for? Pick up to 3
2 When are you going?
3 How long do you have?

No AI, no tracking. Places are scored with the simple rules explained below, using only the data on this site.

How the matching works

Simple rules you can check

Every place has interests it suits, the months it's best in, and how many days it usually takes. The finder adds up points for each place and shows the highest scores first, with the reasons.

Places that share none of your interests are left out. If two places tie, the one that matches more of your interests comes first.

What we checkPoints
Each interest you picked that the place offers+3 each
Your month is in its best season+3
Your month is just before or after the season+0.5
Your month is outside the season−4
Its usual trip length fits your days+2
It's shorter than your trip (pair it with somewhere nearby)+1
It needs more days than you have−3
