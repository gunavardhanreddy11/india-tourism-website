About

About Safarnama

Safarnama means “travelogue” in Hindi and Urdu. This site was built for the PromptX hackathon on top of the starter website we were given.

What it is

A trip finder for India. Instead of listing monuments, it answers the questions people actually have: where should I go, when, and for how long? You pick what you like, when you can travel and how many days you have, and it suggests places that fit.

How it's built

  • Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks or libraries.
  • All content lives in one file, data.js. Every page, card, chart and calendar is built from it, so adding a place means adding one entry.
  • The shortlist is saved in your browser. There are no accounts, forms or tracking.
  • Fonts from Indian type foundries: Rozha One (Indian Type Foundry) and Mukta (Ek Type).
  • Photos from Wikimedia Commons, credited below.

Honest by design

No fake reviews, ratings, user counts or made-up statistics. Festival dates are given as ranges because many follow the lunar calendar. The trip finder uses simple scoring rules that are shown on the page.

What we kept from the starter

The original sections (destinations, culture, gallery and contact), the button that takes you to the destinations, and the three starter destinations, Agra, Jaipur and Kerala, which now form the first-trip route on the home page.
