# 🚀 PromptX – Discover India

## 🌏 Problem Statement

India has a rich combination of **historical monuments, cultural heritage, natural attractions, festivals, local food, and traditions**, but tourists often struggle to find all this information in one simple and engaging platform.

### 🎯 Challenge

Design and develop a website that promotes **Indian Tourism and Heritage** by helping users discover:

* 🏛️ Historical & Heritage Places
* 🗺️ Tourist Destinations
* 🍛 Local Food & Culture
* 🎭 Festivals & Traditions
* 📸 Travel Experiences
* 💡 Travel Information

The website should be **informative, visually appealing, easy to navigate, and engaging**.

---

## ⏱️ Competition Task

You have **60 minutes** to modify and improve the provided starter website.

Use the given **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** files as your starting point and build your own creative tourism website.

### You can modify:

* Website layout
* Colors and typography
* Images
* Content
* Navigation
* Sections
* Destination cards
* Animations
* Interactive elements
* Responsive design
* JavaScript functionality

You are encouraged to add your own creative ideas.

---

## 💻 Technologies
